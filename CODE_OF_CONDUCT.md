# Code of Conduct

PagePet Reader is a small open source project built around dependable reading on the Xteink X3. Everyone who participates is expected to help keep the project focused, welcoming, and useful.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Describe problems with evidence, steps to reproduce, and the hardware context when possible.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and make room for different experience levels.
- Give credit to contributors and respect the licenses of code, fonts, and other assets.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, or deliberate disruption.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Spam, impersonation, or submitting knowingly misleading reports.
- Any other conduct that would make participation unsafe or unreasonable.

## Reporting and enforcement

For a sensitive report, use GitHub's private reporting tools from the repository or contact the repository administrators through [t.me/pagepet_reader](https://t.me/pagepet_reader) with **security** or **conduct** in the first message. Do not open a public issue for private information.

Reports are reviewed by the repository administrators. They may remove comments, restrict participation, or close contributions that violate this policy. Retaliation against a good-faith report is not accepted.
